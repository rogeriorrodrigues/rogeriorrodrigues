### 🔬 What I'm working on

#### AI-Powered Clinical Simulation — UFSC × University of Porto

My current research project explores how Artificial Intelligence can transform clinical education for nursing students. We're building a **real-time AI feedback system** for clinical simulation scenarios, grounded in structured teaching methodologies and learning nodes.

The architecture combines:

- **FHIR R4 (HL7)** as the interoperability backbone — every patient, observation, and clinical event is modeled as a FHIR resource, making the system standards-compliant and portable across healthcare platforms.
- **Synthea** for synthetic patient generation — realistic clinical histories with conditions, medications, allergies, and encounters, all in FHIR format. No real patient data needed.
- **Generative AI with real-time feedback** — the system evaluates student actions during simulation, compares them against expected clinical pathways defined as learning nodes, and provides contextual feedback while the simulation is still running.
- **e4Nursing platform integration** — an international partnership with the University of Porto (Portugal), connecting our AI layer to their established clinical simulation environment used by nursing schools.

> The goal is not to replace instructors, but to give every student a personalized, evidence-based learning companion that scales.

**Tech stack:** Python · FastAPI · LangChain · Azure AI · FHIR R4 · Synthea · Docker · PostgreSQL
